EDITH is a personal project — a fully custom AI operating system built from scratch. No templates, no boilerplate. Every line of code written to solve real daily productivity problems. Built with Node.js, React, MySQL, and the Anthropic Claude API.

🤔 What is EDITH?

EDITH is not a chatbot. It's a personal AI operating system — a digital twin that knows your communication style, remembers your decisions, manages your tasks, reads your emails, monitors your Slack, and queries your business data.

Unlike generic AI assistants, EDITH is designed to be deeply personalized. It learns your preferences over time, stores long-term memories across conversations, and gets smarter the more you use it.

The core idea: what if your AI assistant actually knew you — your role, your priorities, your VIP contacts, your communication style — and acted as a true second brain rather than a general-purpose tool?
